# Contributor Licensing Policy

Forks, issues, and pull requests remain welcome.

Because codex-app-web is source-available for noncommercial use and may also be licensed commercially, non-trivial external code contributions must not be merged until the maintainer has a reviewed Contributor License Agreement (CLA) on record that preserves the project's ability to offer both community and commercial licensing options.

The repository also requires Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) sign-off for non-trivial external contributions. DCO sign-off documents contribution provenance but does not replace the CLA requirement.

Contributors retain copyright in their contributions unless a separate written agreement explicitly states otherwise.

Documentation-only typo fixes and similarly trivial contributions may be handled without a CLA at the maintainer's discretion, subject to applicable law and repository contribution rules.

The draft CLA template in [CLA.md](CLA.md) requires legal review before operational adoption.
